# Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second

One sentence, 147 characters, pitching a startup so strange it sounds like a joke — yet specific enough to see the customers and the money:

> We rent out other people's silence by the hour: tired strangers pay $5 to sit in a stranger's empty guest room and do absolutely nothing, together.

The odd thing sold: silence by the hour, in a stranger's empty guest room. Who pays: tired strangers, $5 a time, to sit and do absolutely nothing — together. Why it holds together: empty guest rooms already exist and cost their owners nothing to lend, and exhausted people will pay a little for permission to simply stop, in company.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, no network, no wall clock) reads the line from `entry.txt` in the repo root and writes it verbatim to `entry.txt` in the output directory given by `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`), reading the repo from `MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR` (default `/bot`). The output is exactly the line, nothing else.
